More Techniques

Few-Shot Prompting

Give 1–2 examples inside the prompt to show the AI what you want. This teaches the AI by demonstration.

Example:

Input: Sales = 100, Cost = 60 → Output: Profit = 40

Now calculate: Sales = 250, Cost = 175

Works great for: Coding, Math, DAX formulas, Formatting, Resume points

Chain-of-Thought

Ask the AI to think step by step instead of jumping to answers. This forces logical thinking and better explanations.

Use phrases like:

  • "Explain your reasoning"
  • "Break it into steps"
  • "First analyze, then provide the final answer"

Refinement Prompts

Never stop at the first answer. Use follow-up commands to improve and refine the output.

Powerful refinement commands:

  • "Make it simpler"
  • "Give a real-life example"
  • "Convert this into interview format"
  • "Shorten to 3 key points"
  • "Add a practical example"
  • "Explain like I'm a beginner"

Negative Prompting

Tell the AI what to avoid. This is powerful for controlling tone, style, and content.

Examples:

  • "Avoid technical jargon"
  • "Do not use emojis"
  • "No long paragraphs"
  • "Do not skip steps"
  • "No assumptions"

Perfect for: Exams, reports, professional emails

Prompt Patterns You MUST Know

  • Learning: "Teach me X as a beginner, use examples, then give practice questions"
  • Interview: "Act as an interviewer and ask me questions, then evaluate my answers"
  • Project: "Improve my project description to be resume-ready with measurable impact"
  • Debugging: "Identify errors in my code, explain why, and provide a corrected version"
  • Dashboard: "Suggest KPIs, visuals, and layout for a dashboard"

Prompting for Your Career

Use these templates based on your field:

  • Data / BI: "You are a data expert. Create KPI ideas and suggest visualizations for this dataset"
  • Python / ML: "Explain this ML concept simply, then show a Python example"
  • Final Year Project: "Improve my project abstract to sound professional and academic"
  • Job Search: "Improve this resume point to highlight impact and metrics"

Pro Tip

Prompt engineering is a conversation, not a one-time request. Keep refining and asking follow-up questions to get better results!
