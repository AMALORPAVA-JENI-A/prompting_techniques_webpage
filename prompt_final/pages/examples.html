Real-World Example: Sales Dashboard Analysis

Let's see how to build a perfect prompt step-by-step. We'll use a scenario: creating a sales dashboard for a retail company.

Step 1: Define the Role

Prompt: "You are a senior data analyst and business consultant with expertise in retail analytics."

Why: AI knows to act like an expert and adjust terminology accordingly.

Step 2: Define the Task

Prompt: "Analyze sales data for the past year. Generate actionable insights, suggest KPIs, and recommend visualizations."

Why: Gives AI a specific, clear objective.

Step 3: Provide Context

Prompt: "The data contains monthly sales, product categories, regions, and profit margins. The audience is management with moderate technical knowledge."

Why: AI tailors answers to the audience and dataset.

Step 4: Add Constraints

Prompt: "Use simple language. Provide 5 insights maximum. Each insight must have an explanation. Avoid assumptions about missing data."

Why: Controls output quality and prevents rambling.

Step 5: Specify Output Format

Prompt: "Format output as: numbered list for insights, table for KPIs, and 3 chart suggestions for each insight."

Why: Makes AI output structured and actionable.

Step 6: Use Chain-of-Thought

Prompt: "Before giving the final answer, explain your reasoning step-by-step."

Why: Ensures logical and traceable output.

Step 7: Add Negative Prompting

Prompt: "Do not use long paragraphs. Do not use emojis. Do not skip steps."

Why: Prevents unwanted content and ensures clarity.

Step 8: Show Examples (Few-Shot)

Prompt: "Example format: Insight: December had highest sales. Reason: Holiday campaigns. Chart: Column chart."

Why: Shows AI exactly what you want.

Step 9: Refinement Prompts

After getting the first response, use follow-up prompts:

  • "Simplify this for non-technical audience."
  • "Convert KPI table into Power BI measures."
  • "Add marketing recommendations for each insight."

Why: Improves and customizes the output.

Step 10: Validation Prompt

Prompt: "Review your answer and highlight any assumptions or uncertainties."

Why: Ensures AI self-verifies and acknowledges limitations.

Step 11: Create Executive Summary

Prompt: "Summarize all insights in a 1-page executive dashboard plan. Include insights, KPIs, charts, and layout recommendations."

Why: Converts all output into actionable deliverables.

Final Result

Following this process, AI will provide:

  • Step-by-step reasoning
  • 5 clear, actionable insights
  • KPI table with definitions
  • Chart suggestions
  • Executive summary ready for dashboard creation

Key Takeaway

Great prompts are like conversations. Start with the basics (role, task, context), add constraints and format, then refine based on output. Each follow-up makes the result better!
