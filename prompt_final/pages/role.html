Role

What is a Role in Prompt Engineering?

A role defines who the AI should act as while responding. It sets the AI's expertise, perspective, tone, and depth of explanation.

How Role Prompting Works

The role is usually stated at the beginning of the prompt in a simple format:

  • Format: "You are a [specific role]"
  • The AI automatically adjusts:
    • Terminology and vocabulary
    • Explanation level and depth
    • Priorities and focus areas

Common Types of Roles

  • Expert roles: Data analyst, software engineer, Power BI expert, marketing strategist
  • Teaching roles: Beginner tutor, instructor, mentor, subject matter expert
  • Perspective roles: Business stakeholder, manager, end user, client
  • Multi-role prompts: Combining technical and business viewpoints

Best Practices

  • Be specific about the role
  • Match the role to the task
  • Combine role with a clear task and output format
  • Use realistic and relevant roles

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not defining a role
  • Using vague or broad roles
  • Assigning conflicting roles
  • Expecting expert output without specifying expertise

Example

Without role:

"Explain data visualization"

With role:

"You are a data visualization expert. Explain best practices for executive dashboards"

Role Prompting Template

You are a [role].
Task: [what you want to achieve]
Context: [background information]
Output format: [bullets, steps, table, etc.]
