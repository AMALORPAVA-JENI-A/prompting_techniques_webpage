Rules

What are Rules?

Rules are instructions that define constraints, limits, and structure for the AI response. They help control how the AI responds by setting clear expectations about tone, format, and content.

Key Questions Rules Answer

  • How should the AI respond?
  • What format should it use?
  • What tone or style to follow?
  • What constraints should apply?

What to Include as Rules

  • Tone: Formal, casual, friendly, technical, or professional
  • Length: Short, detailed, specific number of sentences or words
  • Formatting: Bullet points, table, JSON, paragraph, steps
  • Constraints: Prohibited content, style guidelines, or restrictions

How to Write Rules Clearly

  • Use simple and straightforward language
  • List each rule separately for clarity
  • Be specific about output expectations
  • Use examples to illustrate rules when needed
  • Keep rules concise and actionable

Best Practices

  • Make rules consistent with the task and role
  • Include formatting instructions for clarity
  • Avoid contradictions in rules
  • Use positive language (what to do, not just what not to do)
  • Combine rules with context for better results

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Vague rules that don't specify expectations
  • Missing formatting instructions
  • Overloading rules with too much detail
  • Contradictory or conflicting rules
  • Forgetting to include tone or length requirements

Example

Without rules:

"Give me a list of KPIs."

With rules:

"Give me a list of KPIs in bullet points, max 5 items, formal tone, suitable for executives."

Simple Rules Template

Role: [role]
Task: [what to do]
Rules: [format, tone, length, constraints]
Output: [format]
