Common Mistakes

1. Vague Prompts

❌ Mistake: "Explain machine learning"

❓ Why it fails: No clear goal, difficulty level, or output structure

✅ Better: "Explain machine learning for a beginner using real-world examples in 5 bullet points"

2. No Role Assigned

❌ Mistake: "Create a dashboard"

❓ Why it fails: AI doesn't know what kind of expert to act as

✅ Better: "You are a Power BI expert. Create a sales dashboard for management"

3. Asking Too Many Things at Once

❌ Mistake: "Explain DAX, give formulas, show visuals, make it simple, and give interview questions"

❓ Why it fails: Too many goals result in shallow, confusing answers

✅ Better: Break into steps - first explain DAX, then give examples, then provide interview questions

4. No Context Given

❌ Mistake: "Is this KPI good?"

❓ Why it fails: AI doesn't know the business or audience

✅ Better: "Is this KPI good for a retail sales dashboard used by management?"

5. Not Controlling Output Format

❌ Mistake: Receiving long paragraphs with mixed explanations

✅ Better: "Use headings and bullet points only. Provide a short summary at the end"

6. Trusting the First Answer Blindly

❌ Mistake: Copy-pasting answers without checking

❓ Why it's risky: AI can give confident but incorrect answers

✅ Better: "Verify this answer and list possible mistakes or assumptions"

7. Not Asking for Examples

❌ Mistake: Only theoretical explanations

❓ Why it fails: Hard to understand and apply

✅ Better: "Explain with a real-life example and sample data"

8. Ignoring the Audience Level

❌ Mistake: "Explain PCA"

❓ Why it fails: Too complex or too basic

✅ Better: "Explain PCA to a non-technical manager using a simple example"

9. Repeating the Same Prompt Again and Again

❌ Mistake: Asking the same question multiple times

✅ Better: Refine instead - "Simplify this", "Shorten it", "Add an example"

10. No Constraints Given

❌ Mistake: No limits or rules

❓ Why it fails: Output becomes long and unfocused

✅ Better: "Explain in under 150 words and avoid technical jargon"

11. Asking AI to "Think" Without Direction

❌ Mistake: "Think carefully and answer"

❓ Why it fails: Too vague

✅ Better: "Analyze step by step, then give the final answer"

12. Giving Too Many Examples

❌ Mistake: Including 5-10 examples in one prompt

❓ Why it fails: Confuses the AI

✅ Better: Use 1 or 2 clear examples only

13. Not Using Negative Prompts

❌ Mistake: Only telling AI what to do

✅ Better: "Do not assume missing data. Do not skip steps. Avoid buzzwords"

14. Expecting AI to Guess Your Meaning

❌ Mistake: "You know what I mean"

❓ Why it fails: AI cannot read your mind

✅ Better: Be explicit - "Use sales data from a retail business scenario"

15. Mixing Learning and Final Output

❌ Mistake: Teaching and asking for final solution at the same time

✅ Better: First teach the concept, then generate the final solution

16. Not Resetting Context

❌ Mistake: Long conversations causing confusion

✅ Better: "Ignore previous responses and answer this question fresh"

17. Opinion Questions Without Criteria

❌ Mistake: "Which is best?"

❓ Why it fails: No evaluation rules

✅ Better: "Compare based on performance, cost, and scalability"

18. Copying Viral Prompts Blindly

❌ Mistake: Using fancy templates without understanding

✅ Better: Understand why the prompt works, then customize it

19. Using AI for Wrong Tasks

❌ Wrong use: Exact calculations, medical/legal decisions

✅ Good use: Reasoning, drafting, explanations, idea generation

20. Not Turning Output into Action

❌ Mistake: Reading answers passively

✅ Better: "Convert this into a checklist or step-by-step plan"

The Biggest Mistake of All

❌ Wrong mindset: Treating prompt engineering as typing tricks

✅ Right mindset: Prompt engineering = clear thinking + clear instruction
