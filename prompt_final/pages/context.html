Context

What is Context in Prompt Engineering?

Context means giving background information to the AI. It helps the AI understand why and for whom the task is done. Context answers key questions that guide the AI's response.

Key Questions Context Answers

  • Who is this for?
  • What is the situation or scenario?
  • Why is this needed?
  • What are the constraints or requirements?

What to Include as Context

  • Target audience: Student, beginner, manager, end user, executive
  • Domain or field: Data science, business, education, healthcare, finance
  • Purpose: Learning, project development, interview preparation, decision-making
  • Situation or scenario: Sales data, final year project, dashboard, customer analysis

How to Write Context Clearly

  • Keep it short and simple
  • Share only important and relevant background information
  • Avoid unnecessary details or jargon
  • Place context after the task for clarity
  • Be specific about the scenario or problem

Best Practices

  • Add context when the task can be interpreted in many ways
  • Use real-world scenarios and examples when possible
  • Match context with the task goal and objective
  • Keep sentences easy to understand for the audience
  • Provide enough detail to guide but not overwhelm

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not giving any context at all
  • Providing too much information or rambling
  • Using unclear or confusing background information
  • Assuming the AI already knows the situation
  • Adding irrelevant details that don't help the task

Example

Without context:

"Explain KPIs"

With context:

"Explain KPIs for a sales dashboard used by managers to track team performance and quarterly targets"

Simple Context Template

You are a [role]
Task: [what to do]
Context: [background or purpose]
Output: [format]
